What makes it unique

ATS scoring + profile check — on LinkedIn, with your keys

Most LinkedIn tools stop at bookmarks. CareerCraft scores each role against your CV / profile before you apply — then keeps that score on the Job Tracker row.

ATS analysis on the job page

Know the fit before you apply

  • Compatibility % injected on LinkedIn job pages
  • Must-have vs nice-to-have requirements
  • Skills, experience, education, keywords, responsibilities
  • Strengths, gaps, and a plain-language summary
  • Soft-adds the role to Job Tracker with the score

Profile check

Feed the score with your real profile

  • Upload CV / resume (PDF, DOC, DOCX, TXT)
  • Paste LinkedIn profile text by section
  • Auto extract while you’re on your LinkedIn profile
  • Completeness meter (name, headline, experience, skills)
  • Stored locally — powers every ATS match

Casper AI

Post analysis, discuss with AI, and a real chat box

Casper isn’t a buried settings page — it lives on LinkedIn. Analyze any post, open a chat to discuss strategy or replies, and keep the conversation history on your device.

Post analysis with AI

Ghost icon on feed posts opens Casper with that post’s context — tone, hooks, what’s working, and what to improve.

Discuss with AI

Ask follow-ups in the floating chat: reply angles, connection notes, career strategy, or “rewrite this softer.”

Chat box on LinkedIn

Minimize, expand, new chat, and history. Light/dark theme. Retention limits so storage stays tidy. Opt-in only.

Your key, your provider

Uses the same local AI key as ATS (Gemini, OpenAI, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, or Qwen) — no CareerCraft chat server.

Hands-on

How to use CareerCraft

Install the ZIP

Download the release → unzip → chrome://extensions → Developer mode → Load unpacked.

Add an AI key

Settings → AI API Keys → Gemini, OpenAI, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, or Qwen. Keys stay local.

Load your profile (unique)

Account → upload CV, paste LinkedIn text, or Auto extract — this powers ATS match %.

Run ATS on a job (unique)

Open a LinkedIn job → see compatibility breakdown → role lands in Job Tracker with the score.

Save searches & alerts

Save up to 5 searches → enable notifications when counts rise → use Last 1 hour for freshest roles.

Ask Casper on a post

Click the ghost → post analysis, discuss replies in the chat box, or start a new chat from history.

Feed & write

Feed discovery / Jobs to review · format & paste images · filter Saved items.

Job Tracker

Your LinkedIn job board — without another SaaS

Every viewed, scored, alerted, and feed-captured role in one local board. Status pipeline, export, and employer-kind labels — so you stop guessing what you already opened.

Status pipeline

New → Viewed → Applied → Interview → Confirmed / Rejected / Expired / Archived. Stars for favorites.

ATS scores on the row

See fit % beside title and company before you burn a cover letter.

Source filters

Filter by ATS, alerts, feed, job board, or opened-only — plus employer kind (agency / direct / board).

Export & bulk actions

Export / copy visible or selected CSV. Bulk select and delete when the list gets noisy.

Alert + feed ingest

Saved-search rises and timeline hiring posts soft-add into the same Tracker.

Details when you need them

Applicants, company, outreach tip, and deep links back to LinkedIn — all local on your machine.

FAQ

Questions people actually ask

Is it free?

Yes. Free and open source. You only pay your AI provider if you use ATS or Casper. Donations are optional.

Where do my API keys go?

Chrome extension storage on your device. No CareerCraft key vault.

Do alerts spam me every check?

No. Job alerts notify when a saved search’s total job count goes up — not on every quiet check. First enable sets a quiet baseline.

Will it work when LinkedIn isn’t open?

Saved-search checks and notifications can run via extension alarms. Feed widgets need you on LinkedIn.

Chrome Web Store?

Install today via GitHub Releases (Load unpacked). Star the repo to follow a Store listing later.

Is this a scraper / auto-apply bot?

No. It enhances pages you visit and flows you configure. Not bulk people scrape, not auto-apply.

Download the latest release, load it in Chrome, and give the repo a star if it helps. Feedback and coffee always welcome.
