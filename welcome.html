Welcome to CareerCraft AI

Casper-powered tools for LinkedIn jobs, ATS scoring, formatting, and saved searches — stored locally on your device.

1. Add an AI key (optional)

Open Settings → AI API Keys to connect Gemini, OpenAI, or OpenRouter. Keys stay in your browser; never uploaded to CareerCraft servers.

2. Use LinkedIn as usual

Format posts, paste images, analyze jobs with ATS, and use the Last 1 hour filter on job search.

3. Save searches & alerts

Save up to 5 job searches and optionally enable desktop notifications for new matches.

4. Enable Casper when ready

Turn on Casper in Settings for in-page chat and post analysis icons.
