📌 How to use:
1. Open LinkedIn and click "Start a post"
2. The formatting toolbar will appear automatically
3. Select text and click formatting buttons
4. Paste images directly with Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on Mac)
5. View job pages for AI-powered ATS analysis
6. Visit "Jobs Search Page" and use 1 hour filter to get recent jobs
7. Perform saved job searches from the popup to get quick results
8. Visit "My Items → Saved Posts" to use filters
